## The distinction this page exists to make clear

Recording and managing prescriptions and documents **inside CPMS** is the only capability this page introduces. It does **not** establish, and the page never claims: a connection to a national e-prescription system, automatic transmission of a prescription to anything outside CPMS, or any insurer, pharmacy, ministry or national health-network integration. A reasonable buyer meets the distinction four times, each in different words and a different role: a hero scope note next to the primary CTA, a labelled clarification panel in the prescriptions section («تمایز صریح»), the compact «what this does not mean» block, and the FAQ. The page also states, as a scope line rather than a promise, that it makes no claim about file format or size, signing or stamping, or external sharing; those are questions for the demo.

## Composition

The centre of this page is a **context ledger**: a relationship sheet whose rows read «layer | what it means here» top to bottom, with only the label/meaning pair reflowing (side by side above tablet, stacked below). Every scope clarification shares one **boundary language** (subtle tint plus accent inline-start rule, a text label, reading-size type), so a boundary looks like a boundary wherever it occurs and never relies on colour alone.
